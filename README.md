**UNM-ENG 220 - Team 25 - Energy Reliability Project** 

Team Members: Jeremiah Dacpano, Milo Jacobs, Brian Huynh, Ryan Nguyen, Jazmyne Romero

**About:**
We were tasked to find a way to improve the energy in Cuba NM. We found out that Cuba relies on Jemez Mountain Power as their utility provider, we were able to find data from the EIA that suggests Jemez Mountain Power struggles with power reliability since they only purchase power instead of generating it. Purchasing power from Tri-State Generation requires extremely long power lines that travel from the border of the state into the communities such as Cuba NM. This is where we propose an idea of a Waste-to-Energy generation facility that turns landfill waste into usable energy.
